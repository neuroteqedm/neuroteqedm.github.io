About

Neuroteq makes dubstep, brostep, EDM, ambient and other forms of electronic music. The sound and internet music culture of the 2010s have been a major influence on the project.

The project isn’t tied to one genre. If it sounds interesting, it belongs here.

In September 2026, backzone99 became Neuroteq. The name and visual identity changed, while the music and the history stayed part of the same project.

The label

Query Records

Founded by Neuroteq during the backzone99 years, Query Records is an independent label and a home for the project’s releases.

Visit Query Records

History

  1. 2021–2022

    Hyperactive

    The project started in 2021 under the name Hyperactive.

  2. Earlier alias

    Jumperest

    One of the project's earlier names, before the aliases that followed.

  3. 2023

    klexx / klexxon

    A short period using names such as klexx and klexxon, before becoming backzone99.

  4. 2023–2026

    backzone99

    The project developed over the next three years, exploring different sides of electronic music. Query Records was created during this time.

  5. September 2026

    Neuroteq

    A new name, visual identity and direction. The project continued, carrying everything from the backzone99 years with it.
