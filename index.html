About

Neuroteq is the continuation of the project that began as Hyperactive in 2021. After a few name changes, it developed under the name backzone99 from 2023 to 2026.

That period moved between dubstep, brostep, EDM, ambient and other kinds of electronic music, with a lot of inspiration from 2010s sound and internet music culture. Query Records grew alongside it as an independent label.

In September 2026, backzone99 became Neuroteq. New name, visual identity and direction; same project, still changing.
